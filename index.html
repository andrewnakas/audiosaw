03.FAQ

Does my audio actually stay on my device?

Yes. Open this page, switch to airplane mode, then drop a file. It still converts. That's the test, and the whole point of the site.

Why does my browser need a moment to "warm up" the first time?

For most conversions we load a small WebAssembly module (around 3 MB) on first use. After that it's cached and instant. Simple MP3 encoding doesn't need it at all.

Which formats can I convert between?

MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, and audio extracted from MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV. Most common pairs go both directions.

What about DRM-protected files like Apple Music or Spotify?

Those are encrypted and we can't decrypt them. That's a feature, not a bug. Use the original app to export, or buy the unprotected version.

How big can the file be?

Around 500 MB is a safe ceiling on a modern laptop. We can sometimes handle more, but past 1 GB the browser starts running out of memory.

Are you keeping any data?

We have a privacy-respecting analytics ping (page views, no audio data, no personal identifiers) and Google AdSense ads, which you can opt out of with the consent banner. That's it.

Why is this free?

No servers to run means almost zero cost. There's an ad slot or two. No accounts, no upsell, no Pro tier.
