How it works

Your browser already decodes audio. We pair that with a tiny encoder (pure JavaScript for FLAC, or a slim WebAssembly codec for less common formats) and put it behind a drop target. The whole pipeline runs in your tab. The first conversion may take a moment to load the codec; after that it's instant and cached.

Why convert WAV files

WAV files are great for some workflows and frustrating for others. FLAC fixes the common compatibility headaches without losing what matters about your audio.

FAQ

Is the converter free?

Yes — no signup, no daily limit, no watermark.

Does my file get uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser. Open this page, switch to airplane mode, drop a file — it still works.

Can I convert multiple files?

Yes, drop several at once and you'll get a zip back.

How big can the file be?

Around 500 MB on a typical laptop. Past 1 GB the browser starts running out of memory.
