## First comment, posted by you at launch

> Hi Product Hunt 👋
>
> AudioSaw started from one irritation: every free audio converter asks you to
> upload your file to a stranger's server and wait in a queue, for something
> your browser has been able to do locally for years.
>
> So nothing here uploads. There is no backend at all — the site is static
> files, and your own machine does every bit of the work. Turn your wifi off
> after the page loads and it keeps going.
>
> There are 59 tools. The ordinary ones convert between formats. The ones I'm
> actually proud of:
>
> 🎤 **Stem splitter** — real neural source separation (MDX-Net) running on
> your machine, producing a clean instrumental and acapella. Most tools that do
> this charge for it or upload your song.
> 🎚️ **LUFS loudness normalizer** — proper ITU-R BS.1770-4 measurement, checked
> against FFmpeg's reference implementation within 0.1 LU. This is what
> Spotify actually measures, and it is not the same as peak normalising.
> 🎼 **Audio to MIDI** — hum a melody, get a MIDI file you can drag into a DAW.
> 🎛️ **Autotune** — PSOLA pitch correction, subtle or hard-tuned.
> 🐢 **Slowed + reverb** and ⚡ **nightcore** — the two edits everyone wants.
> 🎙️ **Voice recorder** — the most used page on the site.
>
> What it is not: a DAW. No multitrack timeline, no plugins, no project you
> reopen later. Large files are limited by browser memory, roughly 500 MB, and
> less on iPhone. Audio-to-MIDI is monophonic only.
>
> It is free with no account and no ads, and there is no upsell coming — a
> static site with no conversion servers costs almost nothing to run.
>
> I'd genuinely like to know which tool you reached for first, and what you
> expected to find that wasn't there.
