How to install your ringtone

iPhone (.m4r)

  1. Download the .m4r file we make.
  2. Connect your iPhone to a Mac or PC.
  3. On Mac: open Finder → click your iPhone → drag the .m4r into the device window. On Windows: open iTunes → click your device → drag into Tones.
  4. On the phone: Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone. Your new tone is at the top.

Android (.mp3)

  1. Download the .mp3 file.
  2. Transfer to your phone (USB, Google Drive, email-to-self — anything).
  3. Settings → Sound → Phone ringtone → Add ringtone → pick the file.

FAQ

Why 30 seconds max?

iOS ringtones must be under 30 seconds, or they won't import. Android allows longer but 30 s is a sensible ceiling.

What's the difference between .m4r and .m4a?

Identical contents — both are AAC in an MP4 container. The .m4r extension tells iOS to treat the file as a ringtone instead of music.

Can I make a text tone or alert?

Yes — same file. iOS lets you assign any .m4r as a text tone or alert sound after import.

Does my song upload?

No. Everything happens in the browser tab.
